# The nearest dealers: design

9 October 2026 · Sagnik Mukherjee · approved in chat on 9 October 2026 · status: spec, awaiting review

## Why

Two situations end with "go to your nearest dealer":

1. **The issue stays unclear.** The agent has asked its clarifying questions and still cannot tell what is wrong. A dealer can look at the bike.
2. **A verified, in-warranty fault needs a part fitted.** The fault is shown with confidence, the cover is in force, and the replacement part has to be fitted at a dealer.

In both, the customer needs the three nearest EMotorad dealer stores, with each store's full address and its manager's name and phone number. The app decides how many of the three to show.

## Decisions taken (9 October 2026)

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Which stores | Franchise type **Dealers** only (493 active stores today). Not Others, Shunya, Chanakya or distributors. |
| Whose name and number | The store's linked `franchise_manager` login (name, mobile). Where a store has none, the store's contact person (`poc_name`, `mobile`). |
| Distance from where | The **centre of the customer's pin code** to the **centre of the store's pin code**, in a straight line. No coordinates are kept, per the app team's location addendum of 7 October 2026. |
| How the stores reach the customer | Structured store cards on the reply. The app chooses how many to show. The model never writes a phone number. |
| Where the logic lives | In the chatbot, reading OMS production read-only, as the bike lookup does (`tools/oms_db.py`). No OMS change. |

## What OMS holds (checked 9 October 2026, column names and counts only)

- `em_franchise` has 1,001 active stores. Every one has `pin_code_id`, `address`, `poc_name` and `mobile`. Other relevant columns: `customer_name` (the store's name), `address2`, `district_id`, `state_id`, `franchise_type_id`, `is_distributor`, `is_active`, `deleted_at`.
- `latitude` and `longitude` exist on 625 stores, but 549 of those are `geo_source = city_centroid`. They are not used.
- `em_franchise_type.franchise_type_name` gives the store type: Dealers 493, Others 415, Shunya 70, Chanakya 23.
- `em_pin_code (id, pin_code, district_id)`, `em_district (id, district_name)`, `em_state (id, state_name)`.
- Store managers are `em_users` rows with `user_type = 'franchise_manager'` and `related_id` = the store's id (832 stores have one).
  - `em_franchise.manager_id` is not used: on 39 stores it names an EMotorad regional sales manager, not someone at the store.
- OMS has no coordinates for pin codes.

## 1. Pin-code centres

A new file, `src/emotorad_ai/data/pincode_centres.csv` (outside `knowledge/`, which engineering does not edit; the image copies `src/`): `pincode,latitude,longitude,basis`. It has one row per pin code India Post knows, with values to 4 decimal places.

- **Built by** `scripts/build_pincode_centres.py` from the India Post "All India Pincode Directory" (data.gov.in, Open Government Data Licence India). The source is the same public mirror our `knowledge/_replacement/pincodes.csv` came from (`arobindo/pincode-india-csv`, file `all-india-pincodes.csv`).
- The file is checked in, carries a header comment naming the source, the licence and the fetch date, and is regenerated by the script, never edited by hand.
- **How a centre is worked out:**
  1. Take the post offices' points that fall inside India's bounding box (latitude 6 to 37.5, longitude 68 to 97.5).
  2. Take their median.
  3. Drop points more than 25 km from that median, and take the median again.
  4. That point gets `basis = offices`.
- **A pin code with no usable point** gets the median of its district's centres, `basis = district`. The district comes from the existing directory.
- **Coverage today:** 19,548 of 19,584 pin codes have a usable point. Of the 417 pin codes our dealers use, 409 do. The other 8 fall to `basis = district`, or are left out if their district has no centre either.

A loader, `geo.PincodeCentres`, mirrors `address.PincodeDirectory`: loaded once per process, `centre(pincode) -> Optional[(lat, lon)]`, and `nearest(lat, lon) -> Optional[pincode]`.
- `nearest` scans the centres with a cheap bounding-box filter first.
- It returns None when the nearest centre is over 50 km away. A point at sea or outside India has no pin code.

Distance is the haversine formula in `geo.distance_km`, rounded to whole kilometres for the customer.

## 2. Dealer stores (`tools/dealer_stores.py`)

One read-only query. It reuses the connection setting and options of `tools/oms_db.py`: `EMOTORAD_OMS_PG_DSN`, read-only transaction, 5 s statement timeout, UTC, application name.

```sql
SELECT f.id, f.customer_name AS store_name, f.address, f.address2,
       p.pin_code, d.district_name, s.state_name,
       m.full_name AS manager_name, m.mobile AS manager_mobile,
       f.poc_name, f.mobile AS store_mobile
FROM em_franchise f
JOIN em_franchise_type t ON t.id = f.franchise_type_id AND t.franchise_type_name = 'Dealers'
JOIN em_pin_code p ON p.id = f.pin_code_id
LEFT JOIN em_district d ON d.id = f.district_id
LEFT JOIN em_state s ON s.id = f.state_id
LEFT JOIN LATERAL (
  SELECT u.full_name, u.mobile FROM em_users u
  WHERE u.related_id = f.id AND u.user_type = 'franchise_manager'
    AND u.is_active AND u.deleted_at IS NULL AND coalesce(u.mobile, '') <> ''
  ORDER BY u.updated_at DESC LIMIT 1
) m ON true
WHERE f.is_active AND f.deleted_at IS NULL AND NOT coalesce(f.is_distributor, false)
```

- **Manager:** `manager_name` and `manager_mobile` when present. Otherwise `poc_name` and `store_mobile`. A store with neither is left out.
- **Address:** `address`, `address2`, district, state and pin code, joined with commas, blanks skipped. When `address` already contains the pin code, the pin code is not repeated.
- **Store position:** the store's pin-code centre. A store whose pin code has no centre is left out, and the count is logged as `dealer_stores_unplaced` (no names).
- **Caching:** all stores are loaded at once and kept for 10 minutes. After a failure, reads fail at once for a minute (the breaker pattern of `oms_db`). A failed refresh keeps serving the last good list for up to an hour before answering `oms_unavailable`.
- **Off switch:** with no `EMOTORAD_OMS_PG_DSN`, the tool is not offered (`/health`: `not configured`). Only offline mode, as in tests, uses a fixture list of three made-up stores (the final review, 9 October 2026).
- **The SQL's own behaviour** is pinned by an opt-in test with inline rows, as `tests/test_oms_db_sql.py` does: type filter, distributor and inactive excluded, the manager fallback, the newest manager chosen.

## 3. The customer's area (the app team's location addendum)

Today `api.prepare_turn` replaces the customer's text with "I shared my location..." whenever a location arrives. Under the addendum the app sends a location with a chat's first real message, so that would throw away what the customer typed. The rules below replace it.

- **A message with words, a chip or attachments, plus a location:**
  - the text is kept as typed;
  - the location becomes the nearest pin code (`geo.PincodeCentres.nearest`) and that pin code's district and state (`address.PincodeDirectory`);
  - those reach the runtime as entry metadata `area`, with `source: location`;
  - nothing is said about it to the customer.
- **A message that is only a location** (`text: ""`, no chip, no attachments): unchanged, so the replacement address flow keeps working. It is still turned into the customer's own message (`describe_location`). It also sets `area`.
  - The pin code comes from the local centres instead of Nominatim, so no third party sees the point and the 2-second limit is met.
  - The area name, which only Nominatim gave, is dropped from that message.
- **The runtime keeps the area in the conversation's working state:** `ConversationState.area = {"pincode", "district", "state", "source", "at"}`. It lasts the state's 48 hours, a later location or typed pin code replaces it, and it is never copied into another chat or into the one-line summary of past chats.
- **Coordinates are never kept.** They are used inside `prepare_turn` and discarded. They are not stored in the message, the state, the transcript or logs, and are never sent to the model. A test asserts that no latitude or longitude value appears in any of these.
- **A bad location never fails a message.** A missing, non-numeric or out-of-range value is ignored and logged as `location_ignored` with the reason, not the values.
- **The customer's words win.** The tool takes a `pincode` the customer typed (section 4), and when one is valid it replaces the stored area with `source: typed`.
- **Asking for the area:** while the chat holds an area, the bot never offers `request_location`. Without one, a `no_area` outcome (section 4) makes code add the `request_location` action to the reply, as the website chat already does.

## 4. The tool: `find_nearest_dealers`

- **Who has it:** the customer persona's battery, motor and narrow agents. It is not in the dealer persona's slice; a test asserts it is not offered there. It is read-only, so it needs no idempotency key.
- **Arguments:**
  - `pincode` (optional, string): a pin code the customer typed in this chat. It must be six digits and known to the India Post directory; otherwise the tool answers `bad_pincode`.
  - Nothing else. An extra key is rejected by the registry.
- **Injected:** the conversation's `area`.
- **Outcomes:**

| Outcome | When | What the agent does |
|---|---|---|
| `ok` | an area is known and stores are loaded | says it found the nearest dealers and that their details are below |
| `no_area` | no typed pin code and no stored area | asks for the pin code; code adds the "Share my location" button |
| `bad_pincode` | the typed value is not a pin code India Post knows | asks for the pin code again |
| `oms_unavailable` | stores cannot be read and nothing cached | apologises and gives the customer care contact |

- **What `ok` returns to the model:** for each of the 3 nearest stores, only `store_ref` (`D1`, `D2`, `D3`), `store_name`, `locality` (district and state) and `distance_km`, plus `far: true` when the nearest is over 100 km away.
  - The model never sees the manager's name or phone or the street address, so it cannot misquote them.
  - Ties in distance are broken by store name.
- **What `ok` gives code:** the full three, each with `store_ref`, store name, full address, pin code, manager name, manager phone (as stored, shown with its leading `+91` when it has ten digits) and `distance_km`.
  - These are put on the reply as `stores` (section 5) by the runtime, never by the model.
  - Only the last call in a turn counts.

## 5. Delivering the stores

- **`Reply.stores`:** a new list on the reply contract (`contract.Reply`), each entry `{ref, name, address, pincode, manager_name, phone, distance_km}`, empty by default.
- **Amiigo:** the `reply` frame gains `stores`, the same list. This is an addition within v1: the contract already says the app ignores keys it does not know.
  - A contract addendum, `docs/contracts/amiigo-support-chat-stores.md`, describes the field, an example and how to render a card: name, address, manager, a call button on the phone, distance.
  - The app shows as many as it chooses, in the order given (nearest first).
- **History:** the stores are saved on the bot's transcript turn (`TranscriptTurn.stores`), so `GET …/messages` returns them with that message and the app redraws the cards.
- **Website chat:** `MessageOut` gains `stores`, and `web/emotorad-support-chat-dev.html` renders all three as cards under the reply, with the phone as selectable text and a `tel:` link.
- **Erasure:** the stores are business contact details, not the customer's data. They are erased with the conversation like any transcript turn.

## 6. When the agents use it

These are prompt rules, added to the battery, motor and narrow agents' prompts. The model decides when; the tool is read-only, so no code guard is needed beyond the persona slice.

1. **The issue is still unclear after the agent's clarifying questions.** Offer a dealer visit, call `find_nearest_dealers`, and say the stores are listed below.
2. **A fault was verified with confidence, the part is in warranty and needs fitting at a dealer.** After the ticket is raised, call `find_nearest_dealers` and tell the customer to take the bike to one of them, quoting the ticket reference.
3. **Never in a safety hand-over.** The safety branch runs before any agent, so this needs no rule, but a test asserts no store cards appear on a safety reply.
4. **When `far` is true,** say the nearest dealer is over 100 km away and offer the customer care contact as well.

The one-step rule applies: the agent's text stays short, because the cards carry the detail.

## 7. Privacy and logging

- No coordinates anywhere (section 3).
- Manager names and phones go only into `Reply.stores` and the transcript turn, never into logs or the model's context. Events log the count of stores and the nearest distance only.
- The customer's pin code is personal data at the area level. It lives in the working state for 48 hours and is erased with the conversation.

## 8. Access and sign-offs

- **Database:** read on `em_franchise` (the columns in section 2), `em_franchise_type`, `em_pin_code`, `em_district`, `em_state`, and `em_users (full_name, mobile, user_type, related_id, is_active, deleted_at, updated_at)`.
  - The production login Sachin gave has this.
  - The narrow `ai_chatbot_ro` role, when it is made, needs these grants added; the runbook section lists them.
- **Sachin's yes before real customers:** giving dealer managers' mobile numbers to customers. Sales should tell dealers that riders will call.
- **The app team:** the `stores` addendum (section 5) and their card UI.

## 9. Tests

- **`geo`:**
  - Haversine against known pairs: Pune to Mumbai about 120 km, Delhi to Bengaluru about 1,740 km.
  - `nearest` for a point in a known pin code, and None at sea.
  - The centre builder: median, outlier drop, district fallback, India box.
- **`dealer_stores`:**
  - The manager fallback, the address assembly, unplaced stores left out, the cache and breaker, and serving stale data for up to an hour.
  - The opt-in SQL test with inline rows.
- **`prepare_turn`:**
  - Text plus location keeps the text and sets `area`.
  - A location only behaves as before.
  - A bad location is ignored.
  - No coordinate value appears in the message, the state, the transcript or the log.
- **Registry:** the tool is customer-only, rejects an extra argument, and each outcome is covered.
- **Runtime:**
  - A conversation through `runtime.handle()` that ends with three store cards on the reply and none of their phone numbers in the model's context.
  - `no_area` adds the `request_location` action.
  - A safety report never carries cards.
- **Amiigo socket and history:** `stores` on the reply frame and in history.
- **Website:** `MessageOut.stores`.
- **The whole suite stays green:** `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -t .`

## Out of scope

- Road distance and travel time.
- Opening hours.
- Booking a dealer visit.
- Stores outside India.
- The addendum's optional `accuracy_m` and `captured_at`.
- The addendum's three confirmation questions (history index, resent replies, rate limit), answered separately.
- Store types other than Dealers.

## Rollback

The tool reads only. Rollback is removing it from the three agents' tool lists and redeploying. The area handling in `prepare_turn` can stay, as it only fixes the lost-text bug.
